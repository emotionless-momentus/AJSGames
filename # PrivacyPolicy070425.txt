## 3. Information We Collect

We may collect information about you in a variety of ways. The information we may collect via the Application depends on the content and materials you use, and includes:

* **Account Information:** When you create an account, we link your usage to an anonymized identifier. We require age verification (confirmation of being 13+ or meeting stricter regional requirements, e.g., 16+ under GDPR where applicable) but strive to minimize the collection of directly identifiable personal data for account setup.
* **Location Data (Songlines):** To enable core gameplay features like Songlines, we collect GPS trace data when you use location-dependent features of the App. This data is crucial for tracing paths and interacting with AR elements tied to real-world locations. **Crucially, this GPS data is anonymized and aggregated. Specific paths are not publicly attributable to individual users without explicit consent.**
* **Belief Tagging Data:** You may voluntarily choose to select tags representing your worldview (e.g., faith, ideology, secular viewpoint, or "Prefer Not to Say"). **This information is treated as highly confidential. It is used *solely* within the App to tailor metaphorical language, dialogue prompts via the Ephemeral Guiding Archetype (EGA), and challenge framing to make the experience more resonant. This data is *not* shared externally, *not* used for external profiling, and is firewalled from other datasets where technically feasible.**
* **Dialogue and Interaction Data:** Interactions within Socratic dialogue features (e.g., Confluence Zones), especially those facilitated by the EGA, may be logged anonymously. This data is used solely for system improvement, moderation purposes (enforcing our Code of Conduct), and refining the EGA's facilitation capabilities. **These logs are not publicly linked to user accounts.**
* **Gameplay Data:** We collect data related to your progression (Wu/Wei states, Golden Threads), choices made at Ethical Crossroads, completion of Golden Projection challenges, and interactions with game systems and AR objects.
* **User-Generated Content (UGC):** If you contribute content within the game's systems (e.g., Songline annotations, dialogue contributions, AR Scrolls), you grant DevMap Ltd / AJSGames a non-exclusive, worldwide license to use, display, and distribute this content *within the Acutió ecosystem*, subject always to our privacy protections and moderation policies.
* **Technical and Device Information:** We may automatically collect device information (e.g., device model, OS version, unique device identifiers), IP address, crash data, and general usage statistics for the purposes of providing the service, ensuring security, compatibility, and improving the App. This data is often aggregated or anonymized.
* **Optional Data Integrations:** If features integrating external sources like ResearchGate are implemented and you choose to use them, data interaction specific to that feature may occur based on your explicit opt-in actions.

## 4. How We Use Your Information

Having accurate information permits us to provide you with a smooth, efficient, safe, and customized experience. Specifically, we may use information collected about you via the Application to:

* Create and manage your anonymized account.
* Operate and maintain the Acutió application and its features (including AR, Songlines, EGA facilitation).
* Personalize your in-game experience based on voluntary Belief Tagging (tailoring metaphors and prompts).
* Facilitate player interaction in Confluence Zones and other multiplayer features.
* Monitor and analyze usage and trends to improve your experience with the Application and develop new features.
* Perform moderation, enforce our Code of Conduct and EULA, and ensure the safety and security of our users and Services. This includes using automated tools (like the "Wu-Wei Golden Shield") and human review where necessary.
* Diagnose and fix technical issues.
* Implement age verification and operate the specific safeguards within the "Under-18 Mode" if applicable.
* Respond to your requests and inquiries (when you contact us directly).
* Comply with legal and regulatory obligations.

We are committed to protecting your privacy and limiting the disclosure of your information. We may share information we have collected about you in certain situations:

* **By Law or to Protect Rights:** If we believe the release of information about you is necessary to respond to legal process, to investigate or remedy potential violations of our policies, or to protect the rights, property, and safety of others, we may share your information as permitted or required by any applicable law, rule, or regulation.

**We explicitly state that we do *not* sell your personal information. We do *not* share your specific Belief Tagging data or individual, non-consensual GPS Songline data with third parties for their own marketing or profiling purposes.**

## 7. Data Retention

We will only retain your information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law (such as tax, accounting, or other legal requirements). When we have no ongoing legitimate business need to process your information, we will either delete or anonymize it, or, if this is not possible (for example, because your information has been stored in backup archives), then we will securely store your information and isolate it from any further processing until deletion is possible. Anonymized data may be retained indefinitely for analytical purposes.
